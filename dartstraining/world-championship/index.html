World Darts Championship 2027: a darts practice routine like the pros

Updated 27 November 2026

The World Darts Championship 2027 is back at Alexandra Palace in London from 11 December 2026 to 3 January 2027, this time in the bigger Great Hall, with a break from 24 to 27 December and the final on Sunday 3 January. Three weeks of darts on TV is the best motivation there is to pick up your own darts. Here is a darts practice routine that turns it into real progress – and shows you how to get better at darts at home.

What wins matches at the Ally Pally

World Championship matches are played in sets, so they are long. Nobody hits a 180 every visit. The players who go deep do three things well, over and over:

  • Steady scoring. Not the big visits, but no bad ones. A visit of 41 costs more than a 180 wins.
  • Doubles under pressure. Most legs are decided on the double. The best players keep their checkout rate up when the leg is on the line.
  • A throw that repeats. The same stance, the same elbow, the same release – on the first dart of the night and on the last.

You can practise all three at home, and you don't need hours. If you want to improve at darts, you need a plan.

A 30-minute darts practice routine

  1. Warm up – 5 minutes. Throw at the treble 20 without counting. Find your rhythm and your stance.
  2. Scoring – 10 minutes. Throw 30 darts at T20 and write down your 3-dart average. This is your honest scoring level, and it is the number to beat next time.
  3. Doubles – 10 minutes. Play Bob's 27: start with 27 points, throw three darts at D1, D2 and so on up to the bull. Each hit adds the double's value, a visit with no hit takes it away. Reach the bull without dropping to zero and you have a good doubles day.
  4. Checkouts – 5 minutes. Pick random finishes between 41 and 100 and try to take each one in three darts. Say the route out loud before you throw, like the players do.

Done four or five times a week, this routine does more than two hours of throwing at the 20 once a week. It is the simplest way to get better at darts without a coach.

How to practise doubles

Watch any close match at the World Darts Championship and count how many darts the players need at a double. Top players hit roughly 40% of their attempts at a double; most club players are well below that, and that gap is where legs are lost. If your favourite double is D16 or D20, train it the most – but don't neglect the rest. Bob's 27 shows straight away which doubles let you down.

A simple rule from the pros: always leave yourself a double you can split. From 32 (D16), a single 16 leaves 16 (D8), then 8 (D4). From 40 (D20) a miss into single 20 still leaves D10.

Darts checkouts you should know by heart

  • 170 – T20, T20, bull. The biggest finish in darts.
  • 100 – T20, D20.
  • 81 – T19, D12.
  • 60 – single 20, D20.
  • 40 – D20. The favourite finish of many top players.
  • 32 – D16. Can be split four times.

The more routes you know without thinking, the more of your focus goes into the dart itself.

A throw that repeats

When TV commentators praise a player's throw, they mean it looks the same every time. Most amateur misses come from small changes nobody sees: the elbow drops when you get tired, the body leans forward, the arm stops short instead of finishing at the target, or the rhythm speeds up under pressure.

Practise during the tournament

  • Watch a match, then play a leg. Play a 501 leg against the computer straight after, while the match is still in your head.
  • Copy the winner's double. If a match was won on D10, spend your doubles block on D10 that evening.
  • Measure, don't guess. Keep your average and checkout rate in the app and compare the first week of the tournament with the last. Three weeks is enough to see progress.

Questions players ask

When is the World Darts Championship 2027?

From 11 December 2026 to 3 January 2027 at Alexandra Palace in London, with a Christmas break from 24 to 27 December. The final is on Sunday 3 January 2027.

How long should I practise darts each day?

20 to 30 minutes of focused practice four or five times a week does more than one long session. Split the time between scoring, doubles and checkouts, and keep track of your numbers.

What is Bob's 27 in darts?

A doubles practice game. You start with 27 points and throw three darts at each double from D1 to the bull. Each hit adds the double's value, a visit with no hit takes it away. If you drop to zero or below, the game ends.

How can I get better at darts at home?

Practise with a plan instead of just throwing: measure your 3-dart average on T20, train doubles with Bob's 27 and learn the common checkouts. Checking that your throw stays the same – elbow, body, follow-through and rhythm – fixes many misses.

What is a good 3-dart average for an amateur?

Many casual players average 30 to 40. A pub or league player is often around 45 to 60. Top professionals at the World Championship average close to 100. Compare yourself with your own last month, not with the players on TV.
