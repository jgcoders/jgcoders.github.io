Frequently asked questions

Which formats can Fakturio open?

XRechnung in both syntaxes (UBL and UN/CEFACT CII), ZUGFeRD 2.x and Factur-X in all profiles from MINIMUM to EXTENDED, including PDF files with an embedded XML invoice, and Peppol BIS Billing 3.0. The format is detected automatically.

How do I open an invoice?

From your mail app or files app: tap the attachment and choose “Open in…” or “Share”, then Fakturio. You can also open Fakturio and choose a file there.

A PDF opens with the message that it contains no e-invoice

Only ZUGFeRD and Factur-X PDFs contain the structured invoice data as an embedded XML file. An ordinary PDF invoice (for example a scan or a PDF without embedded XML) is not an e-invoice and cannot be read by Fakturio.

What does the basic check do?

Fakturio checks mandatory fields, the invoice totals, VAT rates and payment details (for example the IBAN check digit) with its own rules and explains any issues in plain words. The basic check is not a full validation under EN 16931 or XRechnung and does not replace the review by your accounting or tax adviser.

Is the exported PDF the legally valid invoice?

No. The e-invoice is the original XML data (or the XML embedded in the ZUGFeRD/Factur-X PDF). The PDF created by Fakturio is a readable representation of that data, for example for filing or forwarding. Keep the original file as well.

What is included in the free version, and what does Pro add?

Free: view and check up to 5 different invoices per calendar month; opening the same invoice again does not count. PDF export with a watermark.
Fakturio Pro (one-time purchase or yearly subscription): unlimited invoices, PDF export and printing without a watermark.

I bought Pro, but the app still shows the free version

Open the Pro screen (for example via PDF export) and tap “Restore purchases”. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used for the purchase. Purchases cannot be transferred between iOS and Android.

How do I cancel the yearly subscription or get a refund?

Subscriptions and payments are managed by Apple or Google.
iPhone and iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Refunds: reportaproblem.apple.com.
Android: Google Play → profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Refunds: via the Google Play help pages.
Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid renewal.

Does Fakturio send my invoices anywhere?

No. Invoices are read only on your device; there is no account and no upload. On Android the app does not even have internet permission. Details: Privacy Policy.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad with iOS / iPadOS 16 or later, and Android phones and tablets with Android 8.0 or later. The app is available in German and English; you can change the language in the app.
