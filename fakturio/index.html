Read e-invoices with ease

Open XRechnung, ZUGFeRD and Factur-X invoices straight from your mail, read them clearly and share them as PDF. Everything happens on your device: no account, no upload.

Coming soon for iPhone, iPad and Android

All common formats

XRechnung (UBL and CII), ZUGFeRD 2.x and Factur-X including PDFs with embedded XML, and Peppol BIS Billing 3.0. The format is detected automatically.

Everything at a glance

Seller and buyer, every line item, VAT breakdown and totals, payment details with IBAN and BIC ready to copy, buyer reference and attachments.

Basic check

Mandatory fields, totals, VAT rates and payment details are checked with the app's own rules and any issues are explained in plain words. It is not a full validation under EN 16931 or XRechnung.

Share as PDF or print

Turn the e-invoice into a readable PDF for bookkeeping, filing or colleagues, or print it directly.

Your invoice stays with you

Invoices are read only on your device. No account, no server, no tracking. On Android the app does not even have internet access.

Free and Pro

Free: up to 5 invoices per month, PDF export with a watermark. Fakturio Pro (one-time or yearly): unlimited invoices, PDF export and printing without a watermark.
