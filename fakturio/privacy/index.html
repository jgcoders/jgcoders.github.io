Privacy Policy

Fakturio for iPhone, iPad and Android · Effective 12 November 2026

In short: Fakturio reads your invoices only on your device. The app does not send invoice content anywhere, has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We, the developer, do not receive any data about you or your invoices from the app.

2. Your invoices stay on your device

When you open an XRechnung, ZUGFeRD, Factur-X or Peppol file in Fakturio, the app reads, displays and checks it locally on your device. The invoice content is never uploaded, sent to a server or shared with us or anyone else.

  • Android: the app does not even have permission to access the internet.
  • iPhone and iPad: the app makes no network requests with invoice content. When you open a file with “Open in…”, iOS places a copy in the app's inbox folder; Fakturio deletes this copy as soon as it has read the file.
  • PDF export: an exported PDF is created in a temporary folder of the app and replaced by the next export. Where the PDF goes after that (mail, files, printer) is your choice in the system share or print dialog.

3. What the app stores on your device

Fakturio keeps only a few settings in the app's private storage on your device:

  • the language you selected in the app;
  • whether Fakturio Pro is unlocked (a cached copy; the App Store or Google Play remains the source of truth);
  • for the monthly limit of the free version: the current month and a short checksum for each invoice opened this month. The checksum is calculated from the invoice number, seller, date and amount; the invoice cannot be reconstructed from it.

This data never leaves your device through Fakturio and is deleted when you uninstall the app. On Android, the app is excluded from system backups. On iPhone and iPad, these settings may be included in your device or iCloud backup, depending on your settings with Apple.

4. Purchases

Fakturio Pro is sold through the Apple App Store (iPhone, iPad) and Google Play (Android). Payment, billing and subscription management are handled entirely by Apple or Google; for this, the app uses Apple's StoreKit and the Google Play Billing Library. We do not receive your name, email address or payment details; the app only learns whether a purchase or subscription is active. Apple and Google process this data as independent controllers under their own privacy policies:

  • Apple: apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Google: policies.google.com/privacy

5. App Store and Google Play

When you find, download or update Fakturio, Apple or Google may process data about you under their own privacy policies; we have no influence on this. As the developer we may receive aggregated statistics from them, such as download numbers, and crash reports if you have agreed to share them with app developers in your device settings. These reports do not contain invoice content and do not allow us to identify you.

6. No analytics, advertising or tracking

Fakturio contains no analytics or advertising tools, no tracking and no third-party software other than the store purchase libraries named in section 4.

7. When you contact us

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR) and delete them when they are no longer needed, unless we are legally required to keep them.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and to object to processing. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. Because the app sends us no data, we normally hold no data about you apart from any messages you send us.

9. Children

Fakturio is a tool for business documents and is not aimed at children. The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

10. Changes

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this policy and change the effective date above.
