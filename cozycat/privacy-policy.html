Cozy Lazy Cats

Privacy Policy · Effective November 23, 2026

In short: Cozy Lazy Cats does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your progress stays on your device.

Who we are

Cozy Lazy Cats ("the app") is a Pomodoro focus timer for iPhone and Android, made by [YOUR NAME] ("we", "us"). This policy applies to the app as distributed on the Apple App Store and on Google Play.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app has no sign-up, does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, or any other personal data, and does not send any information about you or your device to us or to any third party for analytics or advertising.

Information stored only on your device

To remember your progress, the app saves the following on your device, using the standard app storage provided by iOS and Android:

  • your points balance;
  • the outfits and places you have unlocked, and which ones Whiskers is currently using;
  • the IDs of your most recent in-app purchase transactions, so the same purchase is never credited twice.

This information never leaves your device and we cannot access it. It is deleted when you uninstall the app. It may be included in your device backup (iCloud or Google) according to your own device settings.

In-app purchases

The app offers optional point packs. All payments are processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never receive your name, email address, payment card, or billing details. When you buy a pack, the store tells the app only which product was bought and gives a transaction ID, so the points can be added.

Apple and Google handle your purchase under their own privacy policies:

  • Apple: apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Google: policies.google.com/privacy

Third-party services

The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking software. The only external services it uses are the stores' own purchase systems: StoreKit on iOS and the Google Play Billing Library on Android.

Permissions

On Android, the app uses internet, network-state, and Google Play billing permissions. They are used only to talk to Google Play when you open the Points shop or make a purchase. The app works fully offline otherwise. On iOS, the app does not request any permissions.

Tracking

The app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites and does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS or the Advertising ID on Android).

Children

The app is a general-audience productivity app and is not directed at children. Because we do not collect personal information from anyone, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). Purchases are subject to the parental controls of the App Store and Google Play, such as Ask to Buy and purchase approvals.

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the EU and UK GDPR and the California Consumer Privacy Act give you rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Because we do not collect or hold any personal data about you, we have nothing to provide, correct, or delete. You can remove all data the app keeps on your device by uninstalling it. For purchase records, contact Apple or Google directly.

Data security

Your progress is kept in your device's private app storage, protected by your device's own security. Purchases are verified by Apple or Google before points are added.
