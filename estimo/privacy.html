Privacy Policy

Estimo · Last updated 5 November 2026


Estimo is a quoting app made by JGC Krzysztof Martys ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it. In short: what you type into Estimo stays on your phone, and we can't see it.

DATA THAT STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE

Your business details and logo, customers, quotes, line items, room measurements and price list are stored only in the app's storage on your device. Estimo has no account and no server of ours, and this data is never sent to us.

When you share a quote PDF, you choose the app and the recipient, and it is sent by that app, not by us.

When you add a logo, the system photo picker gives Estimo only the photo you choose. Estimo doesn't access the rest of your photo library.

SUBSCRIPTIONS

Estimo Pro is sold through the App Store or Google Play. Payment is handled by Apple or Google, and we never see your payment details.

To check whether Pro is active on your device, Estimo uses RevenueCat, Inc. RevenueCat receives a random identifier created by the app (not your name or email), your purchase and subscription history, and basic technical details such as device model, operating system, app version, country and IP address. This is used only to provide and restore your subscription. RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

WHAT WE DON'T DO

  • No ads and no advertising identifiers
  • No analytics or tracking across apps or websites
  • No selling or sharing of personal data

KEEPING AND DELETING YOUR DATA

You can delete any quote in the app. Uninstalling Estimo removes all of its data from your device. Device backups (iCloud or Google) may contain a copy, under your control.

To have your subscription record deleted from RevenueCat, email us at [contact email] with your App Store or Google Play order number, and we'll ask RevenueCat to delete it.

CHILDREN

Estimo is a business tool and is not directed at children under 16.

YOUR RIGHTS

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK), you have the right to access, correct or delete personal data about you and to complain to your data protection authority. Since we hold no data about you ourselves, most requests are handled by deleting the app; for the subscription record, contact us.

CHANGES

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date.
