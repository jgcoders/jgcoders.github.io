Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy explains what the Darts Training app (“the app”) for iPhone and Android does with your data. In short: your training data stays on your phone, the camera picture is never recorded or sent anywhere, and there is no account.

Who is responsible

The app is developed and published by Krzysztof Martys, the developer named on the App Store and Google Play. Contact: jgcmobileapps@gmail.com.

Data stored on your phone

  • Your training sessions, scores, statistics, custom trainings and settings are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us or to any server.
  • You can export a backup file yourself. Where you send that file is up to you.
  • Deleting the app, or using “Delete all sessions” in the app, removes this data.

Camera (Throw analysis)

  • Throw analysis uses the camera only while you have it open. The picture is analysed on your device to find body points such as your shoulder, elbow and wrist.
  • The picture is never recorded, stored or uploaded. Only the measurements of each dart (for example elbow height and timing) and the body points around each throw are saved, on your device, so you can review and compare throws.
  • You can refuse or withdraw camera access in your phone's settings at any time; the rest of the app keeps working.

Purchases

The Pro upgrade is sold through the App Store or Google Play. Payments are handled entirely by Apple or Google; we never receive your payment details. The app only asks the store whether Pro has been bought.

Advertising

The free version can show ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as device identifiers, IP address and ad interactions, as described in Google's advertising policies. Depending on where you live, you may be asked for consent, and you can limit ad tracking in your phone's settings. Buying Pro removes ads.

No analytics, no account

The app does not use analytics or crash-reporting services and does not ask you to create an account.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights

Because your training data stays on your device, you are in full control of it: you can view, export or delete it in the app. For questions about ads data collected by Google, see Google's privacy policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
