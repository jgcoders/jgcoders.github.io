Questions

Does the app record video of me?

No. Throw analysis looks at the camera picture on your phone, finds your arm and body, and throws the picture away straight after. Only the measurements of each dart are kept, on your phone.

Where do I put the phone?

Upright, side-on to the throwing line, about 2–3 metres away and roughly at elbow height, so your whole body is in the picture. The front camera lets you watch yourself while you throw.

Which phones does it work on?

iPhone and Android phones and tablets. Throw analysis works best on recent phones, which can check more frames per second.

Is it really free?

Yes – all drills, stats for the last 30 days, three custom trainings and 9 darts of throw analysis are free. Pro unlocks the rest with a single purchase.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and nothing is stored on a server. You can export a backup of your sessions whenever you like.

Which languages are available?

English and Polish. The app follows your phone's language.
